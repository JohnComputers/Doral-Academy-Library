Add a book

Point the camera at the barcode on the back of the book.
With Auto-add on, just scan book after book — each one is looked up and added instantly (a beep confirms it). Duplicate copies are welcome and get numbered automatically (Copy 2, Copy 3…). To scan another copy of the same book, wait about 3 seconds between scans. USB scanner guns work too: click the ISBN box and scan. Camera scanning requires https.
